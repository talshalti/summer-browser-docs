What's new in Summer 2.9.0

- Choose from suggested, language-aware RSS and Atom news feeds or add your own. An optional sliding headline strip appears below the address bar and can be adjusted or turned off.
- Find settings with the new Settings search. Configure a panic action to close tabs and reopen your chosen restore tabs; normal tab recovery remains available.
- Open supported local documents in the read-only Office Viewer. AI dictation is available when a transcription service is configured; agent workflows have also been improved.
- Use hover controls for Now Playing in span mode and explore a process-level memory map in the Performance widget.
- See disabled extension badges and clearer recovery notices instead of having extensions silently disappear. This update also improves startup handling and diagnostics.
