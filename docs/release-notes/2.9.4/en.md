What's new in Summer 2.9.4

- Fix a Windows startup crash that could prevent any browser window from opening when loading saved file-indexing settings.
- Keep crash-recovery snapshots up to date after temporary save failures.
- Fix address-bar suggestions when editing after hovering over a result.
- Stop widgets following the pointer after a drag ends inside an embedded frame.
- Keep app credentials and saved-card listings separated between Windows profiles during migration and cleanup.
- Improve tab switching and make Close easier to use in crowded tab bars.
- Recipes adds reusable menus, ingredient suggestions, product photos and nutrition estimates, with improved backup, restore and shopping-list rebuilding.
- Expand AI tools for supported mail, messaging, shopping and food-ordering websites, with stronger checks for repeated actions and better recovery from temporary provider errors.
