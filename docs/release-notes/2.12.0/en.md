What's new in Summer 2.12.0

- Introducing Founder’s Pass on desktop and Android: support Summer with an optional USD 9.99 monthly subscription, including 500 transcription minutes and 100 translation credits per month. Subscribe on desktop; Android supports existing paid accounts.
- Dictate from the address bar or supported webpage fields, with review before insertion. The Speech to Text widget is also available on desktop.
- On desktop, translate text and webpages with explicit cloud translation alongside existing on-device translation.
- Share your pass allowance across linked My Devices on desktop and Android. After linking, each device can use the pass without keeping the original device online.
- Explore the new desktop Founder’s Pass walkthrough and manage your account in Settings → Subscriptions.
