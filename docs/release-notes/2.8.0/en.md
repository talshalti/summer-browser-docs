What's new in Summer 2.8.0

- Send a regular web tab or a supported Clipboard History item to a paired device. The recipient chooses whether to open the tab or copy the item; tab handoff does not transfer cookies, page state, or private tabs.
- Arrange desktop tabs along the top, bottom, left, right, or mirrored edges, with more dependable drag and preview behavior.
- Remove unwanted navigator suggestions and restore them later from the suggestion tray.
- Recover interrupted downloads more reliably and use clearer controls for paused Microsoft Store updates.
- Local AI workflows recover more smoothly from stalled model steps, with a manual Retry action for the current step.
